What's the Ambassador Program?

Yoodle is a free platform where Toronto-area neighbours find work, find help, and build community. We're growing neighbourhood by neighbourhood — and we need local voices to help spread the word.

Brand Ambassadors get featured placement on the Yoodle map, a referral badge, and rewards for every neighbour they bring in. Two tiers, two different ways to represent your community.

Youth

Youth Ambassador

Ages 14–24 · Students, campus reps, community leaders

  • Featured pin on the neighbourhood map
  • Personal referral code with point rewards
  • Ambassador badge on your profile
  • Referral multiplier for every sign-up
  • Dedicated ambassador link to share
Business

Local Business Ambassador

Small businesses, tradespeople, service pros in the GTA

  • Your logo on the Yoodle neighbourhood map
  • Branded map pin with your business info
  • Impression and tap tracking from day one
  • Referral rewards for neighbours you bring in
  • Priority listing in your service area
