Getting Started

Yoodle is a free neighbourhood app that connects people who need help with local odd jobs to people nearby who want to earn money. Whether you need help moving furniture, raking leaves, assembling furniture, or just need an extra pair of hands — Yoodle connects you with trusted, rated people right in your community. It is completely free for everyone, every time.

Yes — 100% free. No service fees, no commissions, no hidden charges. Free to post a job, free to apply, free to message. Yoodle is a community platform built to help people, not to profit from every transaction.

No passwords needed, ever. Sign in instantly with your Google account, or enter your email and we send you a secure magic link — one tap and you are in. Simple, safe, and fast.

Yes. Yoodle works in any browser on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop computer. It is a web app, so there is nothing to install. Just go to yoodle.ca, sign in, and start posting or browsing jobs.

Sign up, post a description of your job, set your budget, and add your neighbourhood location. Within minutes, local people nearby will see your job and apply. You review their profiles and star ratings, pick the right person, and message them directly in the app to confirm details. No agency, no middleman — just real neighbours helping each other.

Post your job and include in the description how many hours or days you need help. Applicants will reach out through the app's messaging system. Yoodle does not process payments. You and your helper agree on a price through the app and settle payment directly — cash, e-transfer, or however you prefer. This keeps things simple, local, and free of platform fees for both sides.

I Want to Earn Money

Sign up on Yoodle and browse jobs posted in your area — view them on a map or as a list. Apply to jobs that match your skills and schedule. Once the poster accepts you, chat in the app to confirm details, complete the job, and get paid directly. The more jobs you complete with great ratings, the more easily you will land future work.

For Small Businesses

Absolutely. Yoodle is a great fit for small businesses that need flexible, on-demand help without the cost and commitment of a staffing agency. Post a job for a few hours, a full day, or a specific one-off task — like event-day help, moving stock, flyer delivery, setting up a display, or general labour. You pay only the person you hire. Yoodle charges nothing.

Yoodle is ideal for short-term, task-based, or occasional help — not for permanent employment. Think of it as your on-demand community workforce for the times you need an extra pair of hands without hiring someone full-time. For recurring work, you can re-hire the same great helper directly through the app.

In most neighbourhoods, jobs receive applications within minutes of posting. Write a clear description, set a fair budget, and you will typically have several applicants to choose from very quickly. The more notice you give in your posting, the more options you will have.

Safety & Trust

Every Yoodle user has a public profile with a star rating built from real, verified jobs. After each completed job, both sides rate each other — so the community holds itself accountable. Review someone's rating and job history before accepting them. We also have a reporting and blocking system for any concerns. For a first-time job with someone new, we recommend meeting in a visible, accessible location.

Yoodle only ever shows your general neighbourhood — never your exact address or precise GPS coordinates. Map pins are slightly offset for additional privacy. Your precise location is only used to surface relevant nearby jobs and is never shared with other users. Read our full Privacy Policy for all the details.

You can report or block any user directly from within the app. Our team reviews all reports. If you have a concern that needs direct attention, reach out to us at privacy@yoodle.ca and we will respond promptly.

How We're Different

Yoodle is free — no service fees, no commissions, ever. It is designed specifically for neighbourhood-scale, informal tasks and real community connections. It is faster and more personal than agency platforms, and more focused on local trust and ratings than classified ad sites. Yoodle's mission is to bring communities together — not to build a business on top of your transactions.

Yoodle works anywhere. Your map automatically shows jobs near your current location. The more people in your area who use Yoodle, the more active your local feed becomes — so spread the word to your neighbours, local groups, and community boards.
