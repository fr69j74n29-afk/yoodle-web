What is Yoodle?

Yoodle is a free app that connects neighbours in Canada for odd jobs and local help. Post a task — yard work, moving help, cleaning, snow shovelling and more — and people nearby offer to help. Or set up a helper profile and find paid work close to home.

You sign in to Yoodle with Google or with a code sent to your email, so you can post jobs, message neighbours and get updates. Posting and applying for jobs is always free, and Yoodle never sells your data. See our Privacy Policy.
