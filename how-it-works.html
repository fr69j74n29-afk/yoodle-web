I need help
📋 Posting a Job
1
Describe what you need
Write a short job title, add a description, set your budget, and pin your neighbourhood on the map. No exact address required — your privacy is protected.
⏱️ Takes about 2 minutes
2
Pick your helper
Local people nearby will see your job and apply. Browse applicant profiles, check their star ratings and completed jobs, and choose the person you feel good about.
⚡ Applications arrive fast
3
Get it done
Chat directly in the app to confirm timing and details. The job gets done, you pay your helper directly — cash, e-transfer, your choice. Then leave a rating to help the community.
💸 No platform fees, ever
Great for
Homeowners · Parents · Seniors · Small businesses · Landlords · Anyone who needs an extra pair of hands
I want to earn
💼 Finding Work
1
Browse jobs near you
Open Yoodle and see local jobs on a live map or scrollable list. Set your search radius and find tasks that fit your skills, your schedule, and your neighbourhood.
📍 Sorted by distance
2
Apply in seconds
Tap to apply. Your profile and star rating go to the poster automatically — no cover letter, no resume, no hassle. Build your rating with every job you complete.
🚀 One tap to apply
3
Do the work, get paid
Chat with the poster to confirm the details. Show up, do a great job, and collect your payment directly — cash, e-transfer, or whatever works. Rate each other and build your reputation.
⭐ Ratings build trust
Great for
Students · Young adults · Anyone with free time · People building work experience · Entrepreneurs building a local client base
