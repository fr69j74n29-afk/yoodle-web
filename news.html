November 10, 2026 Jobs

More ways to find work: Alliance Employment Services jobs are now on the map

Most people who join Yoodle are looking for work, and a neighbour’s help request is only one kind of work. To give you more choice, we’ve started bringing job openings from employment agencies onto the map, right next to the jobs posted by neighbours.

The first is Alliance Employment Services, a Toronto-based staffing agency with more than 20 years of experience in manufacturing, warehousing and logistics, food production, and construction and landscaping. Their openings cover the GTA, with temporary and full-time roles.

  • Agency jobs show as blue pins on the map. Jobs from neighbours stay red.
  • Tap a blue pin to see the job, the pay, the shift and the general area.
  • Tap Apply directly with Alliance to apply on their website. Yoodle isn’t part of the hiring and never takes a cut.
  • The list updates every morning, so filled jobs drop off and new ones show up.

New to Yoodle? Create your free account and open the map to see what’s near you. Run a staffing agency? Email us at support@yoodle.ca.

November 5, 2026 New feature

Available Now: find local help for today or tomorrow, or let neighbours know you’re free to work

Some jobs can’t wait a week. A crew member calls in sick, a delivery shows up early, the driveway is buried after an overnight snowfall, the yard needs clearing before the weekend. And plenty of people nearby are free right now and just need someone to know it. Available Now connects the two. Helpers can mark themselves available for today or tomorrow, and anyone hiring can spot them on the map at a glance.

Need someone for a day? Contractors, businesses and homeowners:

  • Sign in to Yoodle (it’s free) and open the map.
  • Look for helper pins with a gold ⚡ bolt and a pulsing gold ring. Their cards show ⚡ Ready today or ⚡ Ready tomorrow.
  • Tap the pin, check their profile and send a message to set the time, place and pay. Look for the ✓ ID verified badge if you want extra peace of mind.
  • Right now it covers General Labour, Moving & Lifting, Yard & Garden and Cleaning. No fees and no middleman.

Free to work today or tomorrow? Let the community know:

  • Open Profile → ⚡ Ready to work and tap Today, Tomorrow or both.
  • You’ll need an active helper profile with one of the skills above, and notifications turned on so people hiring can reach you fast.
  • Each day switches off on its own at midnight, and we send a quick reminder in the evening so you can stay listed for the next day.
  • It’s free. Verified helpers tend to get hired first, so consider getting ID verified too.

New to Yoodle? Create your free account and set up a helper profile or post your first job.

October 15, 2026 New feature

Introducing ID verification on Yoodle, powered by Stripe

Starting today, any Yoodle member can get ID verified. Verified members show a ✓ ID verified badge on their profile, in chats and on their helper pin, so neighbours can see that a real person with valid ID is behind the account.

Why it matters. Yoodle connects people who often haven’t met before, sometimes for a job at their own front door. Ratings help, but a new helper has none yet. The badge gives posters one more reason to say yes, and gives honest helpers an easy way to stand out. Together with our scam protection and moderation, it makes it harder to hide behind a fake account.

Checked by Stripe. The check is done by Stripe Identity from Stripe.com, the payments company used by millions of businesses worldwide. Stripe compares a photo of your government ID with a live selfie taken on your phone. Yoodle never sees or stores your ID or your selfie; we only receive the result.

How it works:

  • Tap Get ID verified on the + screen or in your Profile.
  • Pay $2.99 + tax on Stripe’s secure checkout. Up to 3 tries are included.
  • Take a photo of your driver’s licence, passport or provincial ID card, then a quick selfie. It takes about 2 minutes.
  • Once Stripe confirms the match, your badge appears right away.

ID verification is optional and Yoodle stays free to post, apply and message. The badge confirms a real person with valid ID owns the account; it isn’t a background check, and display names stay nicknames. Members must be 18 or older to get verified, and the fee is non-refundable. Details are in our Terms, Privacy Policy and FAQ.

October 13, 2026 Update

Keeping Yoodle safe: scam protection and moderation

We regularly review our platform for scams and fraud. Accounts caught scamming are suspended: their jobs and profile come off the map, and they can no longer message anyone.

How to stay safe:

  • Real neighbourhood jobs don’t pay a monthly salary for remote office work.
  • Never pay upfront, buy gift cards, or move the chat off Yoodle for someone you haven’t met.
  • If something feels off, tap Report or Block on the member’s profile or in the ⋮ menu of your chat with them. We review reports within 24 hours.
