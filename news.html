Yoodle News

New features, partnerships and updates from Yoodle, the free app for neighbours who need a hand or want to earn.
October 9, 2026 New feature

Messages from the Yoodle team

When we need to reach you, our message now arrives in its own chat from the Yoodle team in your Messages, separate from your jobs.

  • Reply any time. It comes straight to us.
  • Not needed? Tap Hide this chat.
  • Chats close on their own after 21 days with no new messages.
October 2026 Update

Yoodle is now open across Canada

Yoodle started in Toronto. Neighbours anywhere in Canada can now post a job or set up a free helper profile. The idea stays the same everywhere: help from people who live near you, with no fees and no middleman.

September 2026 New feature

A faster map, Light and Dark themes, and more control over your account

  • New map: smoother and quicker to load, with red pins for jobs and green pins for helpers.
  • Light and Dark: switch with the toggle at the top right.
  • Sign in with a code: choose “Email me a code” and type the 6-digit code. No password needed.
  • Your account, your choice: turn off Yoodle emails or delete your account any time in Profile.
