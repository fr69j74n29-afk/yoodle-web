Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Yoodle ("we", "us", or "our") is a community platform connecting neighbours for odd jobs and local help. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. It applies to the Yoodle website at yoodle.ca and the Yoodle app for iPhone. Please also read our Terms of Use.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Yoodle, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google. You choose the display name other people see.
  • Profile information — anything you add to your profile, such as an "About me" bio and, if you offer help, your helper description, categories and rate.
  • Location — the location you enter or detect when you post a job or set up a helper profile. This can be a street address, and we store it together with its map coordinates. See section 3.
  • Jobs and activity — jobs you post (title, description, budget), jobs you apply for, and their status.
  • Messages — messages you exchange with other users about a job or help request.
  • Ratings, reports and blocks — ratings you give and receive, reports you submit, and users you block.
  • Notification data — if you allow push notifications, a device identifier from our push provider that lets us send notifications to your device.
  • Referral information — your referral code, points, and who joined using your code.
  • Technical data — standard logs kept by our service providers, such as IP address, browser or device type, and timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To run your account and sign you in, including sending one-time sign-in codes by email.
  • To show jobs and helpers near you and connect you with neighbours.
  • To deliver messages and send notifications about them — push notifications if you allow them, and an email if a message stays unread for 30 minutes. You can turn these emails off in your Profile.
  • To show ratings so neighbours can decide who to work with.
  • To review reports, enforce our Terms of Use, and prevent fraud, abuse and safety problems.
  • To maintain and improve Yoodle.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Location Privacy

Your precise location is never shown to other users:

  • Job and helper pins on the map are shifted randomly by up to about 80 metres, and you can choose "List only" so no pin is shown at all.
  • Other users may see an approximate distance, such as "1.2 km away".
  • When you use the locate button, your device's GPS position is used on your device to centre the map and is not saved to your account.

The address and coordinates you save with a job or helper profile are stored in our database so we can show you to nearby neighbours.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. We share information only as follows:

  • With other users — your display name, bio, helper details, ratings, job details, messages you send them, and approximate location as described above.
  • With service providers that help us run Yoodle, only for that purpose:
    • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage
    • OneSignal — push notifications
    • Resend — email delivery
    • Cloudflare — website delivery, security and email routing
    • GitHub Pages — website hosting
    • OpenFreeMap — map tiles (based on OpenStreetMap data); OpenStreetMap — backup map tiles
    • OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service — address search (the text you type in the address box is sent to look it up)
    • Google — sign-in, if you choose "Continue with Google", and web fonts
    • Apple — push notification delivery in the iPhone app
  • If required by law — in response to valid legal process, or to protect someone's safety.

Some of these providers store or process data outside Canada, including in the United States.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app: go to Profile → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, jobs, helper listing, messages, applications, ratings, points and referrals. Jobs you were helping with go back to their poster as open.

Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time until they are overwritten. Report records may be kept where needed for safety or legal reasons. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing us (section 11).

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure sign-in, and database access rules that limit who can read your data. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Yoodle is not intended for anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information — most of it you can edit yourself in your Profile.
  • Delete your account and data (see section 5).
  • Withdraw consent: turn off push notifications in your device settings, turn off emails in your Profile, or delete your account.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

Yoodle uses essential cookies and your device's local storage only — to keep you signed in, remember your theme, save a job draft before you sign in, and remember your last map position. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page with an updated date, and tell you in the app about significant changes. Continued use of Yoodle after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
